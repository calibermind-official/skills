# Writing the content

The queries give you the numbers. Your job is to turn them into words a stakeholder can read in a
few seconds. Write for a VP who has never seen the CRM: plain words, no internal codes, no
campaign naming conventions.

The chart is dense, so text must be short. The length limits below keep it readable at
presentation size. If your format has more room, you can relax them a little, but never let a
label wrap or crowd its neighbors.

## Header line

`COMPANY · DEAL TYPE · CLOSED WON MON D, YYYY · $AMOUNT`, in upper case. Use a short company
name. Round the amount ($60K, $1.2M), and leave it out if it is zero or negative, which happens
on renewals.

- Open opportunity: use the current stage instead of "CLOSED WON ...".
- Closed lost: "CLOSED LOST MON D, YYYY".
- Expansion deal (upsell, renewal, cross-sell): say so in the deal type part, for example
  `EXPANSION`, so a reader knows the timeline includes earlier customer history.

## Title

Default: "From first signal to **closed won**", with the last words emphasized. Use "today" for
an open opportunity and "closed lost" for a lost one. Keep the title short and factual. Do not
borrow slogans.

## Stats (exactly 4)

1. Time from the first signal to the close (or to today), for example "3 yrs 2 mo", "11 mo", or
   "45 days". Label: "from first signal to signature".
2. Total marketing touches (query 5 totals, all lanes). Label: "marketing touches on the
   account".
3. Number of people with touches (query 4). Label: "people in the buying group".
4. Total sales activities including meetings (query 5 totals). Label: "sales activities, incl.
   N meetings". Drop the meeting part if there were none.

Keep each label to one line of about 32 characters.

## Lane labels (one per lane, in lane order)

- **Account lane:** name "Account signals". Underneath, a count summary from the intent and
  account marketing totals, for example "12 intent surges, 4 ads". Leave out any part that is
  zero.
- **Person lanes:** the person's full name. Underneath, their title shortened to about 22
  characters: "VP, Marketing Operations" becomes "VP, Marketing Ops", and "Head of Marketing
  Technology" becomes "Head of MarTech". Keep the meaning and cut filler such as "Global" or
  regional prefixes.
- **Everyone else lane:** name "Everyone else". Underneath, for example "4 more + anonymous",
  based on how many named people are not in key lanes and whether anonymous visitors are present.

## Milestones (3 to 5)

Start from query 7 plus the opportunity's created and closed dates, and pick what tells the
story:

- Always keep the first signal and the close (or "Today" for an open opportunity).
- Keep the first known person when that date is clearly later than the first signal.
- Opportunity creation matters. If a hand raise (demo request, MQL) happened within a few days of
  creation, merge them into one label, for example "Demo booked, opp opens".
- You can add one deal moment from the meetings in query 6, such as a pilot kickoff, a security
  review, or a final presentation, if it is clearly important. Describe it in plain words of
  your own. Never copy a meeting's name.
- Name the first signal by what it was when that helps, for example "First intent signal".
- Keep labels to 22 characters or fewer. If two milestones fall so close together that their
  labels collide, merge them or drop the less important one.

## Key moments (0 to 5, marketing only)

Pick them from query 8 using the rules at the end of `queries.md`. For each, write:

- **Who:** the person's first name.
- **When:** the date, or a date range for a research burst ("Jun 2–6").
- **What:** one plain phrase of 45 characters or fewer about what they did. Use the event name,
  URL, and campaign to work out the asset, then describe it the way a person would say it:
  "Attended the live product webinar", "Downloaded the benchmark report", "Booked a demo from
  the pricing page", "Clicked the launch announcement email".
- **Research burst:** summarize the page count and the kinds of pages, and fold in any high or
  medium touch you merged into it: "40 docs and integration views, security form".

Do not invent details the data does not show. If a name is cryptic and the URL does not help,
describe the type of touch ("Attended a webinar") rather than guessing the topic.

Number the moments 1 to N in date order, with no gaps. The same number marks the moment on the
chart and on its card.

## Source and notes

- **Source line:** "Source: CaliberMind, through MON D, YYYY".
- **Speaker notes** (or a caption, if the format has no notes): 3 to 5 sentences that walk
  through the story in the order of the chart. Say when marketing first showed up, how the
  opportunity started, how sales ran the deal, and how marketing stayed involved. Plain text,
  with no detail-field content and no email text.
